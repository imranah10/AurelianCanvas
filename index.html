Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Aurelian Canvas ("we", "our") sells instant-download printable wall art at aureliancanvas.gumroad.com and operates the Pinterest API application "Aurelian Canvas Poster" for its own Pinterest business account (AurelianCanvas).

What data the app accesses

  • The board list of our own Pinterest account (board names and IDs).
  • The ability to create Pins on our own boards, using our own images, titles, descriptions and product links.

What we store

  • OAuth credentials (App ID, App secret, refresh token) are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. They are never public and never shared.
  • Schedule state (pin IDs, posting log) is stored in our private automation repository.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, read or store personal data of any other Pinterest user.
  • We do not access, follow, message or scrape any other account.
  • We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising or analytics.

Revoke access & data deletion

Access can be revoked anytime on Pinterest: Settings > Security and logins > Apps, then remove "Aurelian Canvas Poster". Stored tokens are deleted on request.
